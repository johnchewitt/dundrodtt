## Corrections & conflicts register (binding on all copy)
1. 1952 Ulster Trophy winner: **Taruffi** (Vandervell Ferrari 375), not
   Hawthorn (Hawthorn was second, Cooper-Bristol). The 1952 RAC TT was
   not held; do not imply no car racing happened in 1952.
2. 1955 TT: Moss/Fitch, 84 laps, **7:03:11** (not 7:03:12), avg 88.32
   mph; Hawthorn's D-Type lap record 4:42 (~94.67 mph).
3. The 1955 notes txt claim "Moss and Fangio finished 1st & 2nd" is not
   used; its graphic crash/Le Mans narrative is not used. Fatalities per
   the context doc: Mayers and Smith at Deer's Leap, Mainwaring at
   Tornagrough; Winterbottom in the 1951 TT.
4. 1954 "£8,000 loss"/sponsorship causation: unverified — keep out.
5. "1950s hairpin", never "Lindsay Hairpin" (post-1965 name). 1965
   changes (bridge bypass, altered hairpin) never shown as the 1950s
   course.
6. "Queen Elizabeth" (with Princess Margaret) in the 1951 setting.
7. 1949 scheme figures are reported estimates, never audited costs.
8. 1950: Ulster Trophy winner Whitehead (Ferrari 125); Powys-Lybbe won
   the handicap contest (Alfa Romeo P3); Moss's win was the September
   TT (Wisdom's XK120, eve of his 21st birthday) — never the August UT.
9. Photos flagged *confirm before final publication* (DU-1951-03,
   DU-1951-04, DU-1952-05, DU-1955-10): ship only with the spreadsheet's
   hedged wording, or owner confirms.
10. Hero-candidate PNG (`Stirling Moss winning last Dundrod race
    1955.png`) has a small viewer icon baked into its top-right corner —
    owner may supply a clean export; frame around it meanwhile.
