## Standing rules

- No AI-generated historical imagery; no photographs of people taken
  from the web; licensed landscape/period-map material only with its
  credit (see CLAUDE.md).
- Captions, alt text and identifications render verbatim from the
  catalogue; hedges stay hedged; review notes never render.
- Unidentified footage is never attributed to a particular year or
  event beyond its holder's own cataloguing.
